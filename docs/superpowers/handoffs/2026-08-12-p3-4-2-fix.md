## New Filters (chain order)

1. `LossLimitFilter` (existing)
2. `MaxLossPerTradeFilter` — rejects when potential loss > max_loss_pct of equity
3. `RiskRewardFilter` — rejects when RR < min_risk_reward (when both SL/target present)
4. `MarginFilter` (existing)
5. `MarginHeatFilter` — rejects when post-trade utilization > cap
6. `UnderlyingConcentrationFilter` — rejects when > N positions per underlying
7. `SectorConcentrationFilter` — rejects when sector > cap of total notional
8. `DirectionConcentrationFilter` — rejects when one direction > cap
9. `MaxPositionFilter` (existing)
10. `StopHitCooldownFilter` — rejects re-entry within cooldown after SL hit
11. `RegimeFilter` (existing stub)

## Key Design Decisions

- **ProposalRiskContext is backward-compatible**: `check()` default `proposal=None` keeps all existing tests/filters valid.
- **Missing data → allow passively** (for MARKET orders): `entry_price=None` or `equity=None` allows the filter to pass, since we can't evaluate. The execution engine always provides a proposal at approve-time.
- **Concentration filters skip trivial cases**: single-sector or single-direction portfolios don't trigger concentration rejections (100% is trivially true, not a concentration problem).
- **RISK_ALERT published on rejection**: `_publish_risk_alert()` emits `Topic.RISK_ALERT` with `{symbol, filter_name, reason, proposal_id}` — the topic + subscribers already existed, only the publisher was missing.
- **Paper engine equity**: `available_margin + total_margin_used` — honest and always available.
- **Settings-backed pattern**: all new filters follow the `_settings_backed` pattern of `LossLimitFilter` — re-read from store on every check, so runtime settings changes are honored.
